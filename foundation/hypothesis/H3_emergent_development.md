# H3: Emergent Development Hypothesis

**A minimal predictive processing organism, operating under free-energy minimization, will exhibit staged cognitive development — moving from sensorimotor binding to object permanence to counterfactual reasoning — without explicit programming of those stages.**

## Key Predictions

1. The organism progresses through at least three identifiable developmental stages.
2. Stage transitions coincide with drops in free energy.
3. The sequence is invariant across random seeds (same order, variable timing).
4. Each stage builds on representations formed in the previous stage.

## Experimental Tests (EXP3, EXP4)

- **EXP3:** Minimal predictive organism — demonstrate emergence of staged development.
- **EXP4:** Cycle bootstrapping — show that the organism can bootstrap its own training curriculum.
- **Success criterion:** Observable staged progression with free-energy gated transitions, invariant across seeds.
