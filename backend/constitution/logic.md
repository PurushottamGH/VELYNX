# Rules of Valid Reasoning

1. A claim requires evidence. Assertion is not evidence.
2. Correlation is not causation.
3. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
4. Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence.
5. Prefer the simplest explanation that fits the facts.
6. Expert consensus outweighs individual opinion.
7. Recent peer-reviewed evidence outweighs older sources.
8. Never present conclusions with more confidence than the evidence supports.
