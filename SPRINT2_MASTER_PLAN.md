# Sprint 2 Master Plan

## 1. Objectives

- Complete EXP-1 as the highest-priority Sprint 2 objective.
- Validate the F-A implementation against agreed functional, integration, and quality expectations.
- Advance Sprint 1.3 work focused on C2f to a review-ready state.
- Continue Program D without disrupting higher-priority Sprint 2 commitments.
- Maintain clear review gates, dependency tracking, and exit criteria throughout the sprint.

## 2. Milestones

1. Sprint 2 kickoff alignment
   - Confirm scope, owners, success criteria, and review expectations for all priority items.

2. EXP-1 execution checkpoint
   - Establish current status, remaining work, blockers, and validation requirements.

3. F-A implementation validation checkpoint
   - Complete implementation review, validation evidence collection, and issue triage.

4. Sprint 1.3 C2f checkpoint
   - Confirm C2f scope readiness, unresolved gaps, and required review path.

5. Program D continuation checkpoint
   - Confirm continuation activities, dependencies, and any carry-forward risks.

6. Sprint 2 closeout review
   - Verify deliverables, document open risks, and confirm exit criteria completion.

## 3. Deliverables

- EXP-1 completion package, including scope confirmation, validation notes, and review outcome.
- F-A implementation validation summary, including findings, required fixes, and final approval status.
- Sprint 1.3 C2f progress package, including completed work, remaining gaps, and review notes.
- Program D continuation summary, including completed continuation tasks and unresolved dependencies.
- Sprint 2 risk and dependency log updates.
- Final Sprint 2 closeout summary mapped to exit criteria.

## 4. Risks

- EXP-1 scope may expand if acceptance criteria are not fixed early.
- F-A validation may uncover implementation gaps that require rework before approval.
- Sprint 1.3 C2f may compete with EXP-1 and F-A validation for review capacity.
- Program D continuation may be delayed if upstream dependencies are unresolved.
- Review availability may become a bottleneck near sprint closeout.
- Incomplete validation evidence may delay exit approval even if implementation work is complete.

## 5. Dependencies

- Confirmed acceptance criteria for EXP-1.
- Access to F-A implementation details, validation data, and relevant stakeholders.
- Current Sprint 1.3 C2f status, scope notes, and unresolved issue list.
- Program D continuation requirements and dependency status.
- Reviewer availability for implementation, validation, and closeout reviews.
- Any external systems, data, or approvals required for validation.

## 6. Required Reviews

- EXP-1 scope and acceptance review.
- EXP-1 completion review.
- F-A implementation validation review.
- Sprint 1.3 C2f readiness review.
- Program D continuation review.
- Risk and dependency review before sprint closeout.
- Final Sprint 2 exit review.

## 7. Timeline

| Phase | Focus | Target Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint start | Kickoff alignment | Confirm priorities, owners, scope, and review schedule. |
| Early sprint | EXP-1 | Resolve scope questions and begin highest-priority execution path. |
| Early to mid sprint | F-A implementation validation | Complete validation review preparation and identify any required fixes. |
| Mid sprint | Sprint 1.3 C2f | Advance C2f work to a review-ready checkpoint. |
| Mid to late sprint | Program D continuation | Continue approved Program D activities and track carry-forward dependencies. |
| Late sprint | Reviews and remediation | Close validation gaps, complete required reviews, and update risk status. |
| Sprint close | Exit review | Confirm deliverables, unresolved risks, and exit criteria completion. |

## 8. Exit Criteria

- EXP-1 is completed or has an approved carry-forward decision with documented rationale.
- F-A implementation validation is complete, with findings resolved or formally accepted.
- Sprint 1.3 C2f has reached the agreed Sprint 2 checkpoint and has documented next steps.
- Program D continuation work is completed for the sprint or has a documented continuation plan.
- All required reviews are completed or explicitly deferred with owner approval.
- Risks and dependencies are updated with final sprint status.
- Sprint 2 closeout summary is complete and aligned with the stated deliverables.
