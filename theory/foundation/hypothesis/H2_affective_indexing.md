# H2: Affective Indexing Hypothesis

**A "soul graph" of affective-concept associations provides a structured inductive bias that improves sample efficiency in concept acquisition compared to tabula-rasa learning.**

## Key Predictions

1. Concepts acquired via affective indexing require fewer examples to reach criterion (≥50% fewer).
2. Affective framing significantly modulates acquisition speed (framed vs. unframed, p < 0.01).
3. The soul graph's structure is reproducible across initializations given the same affective ontology.

## Experimental Test (EXP2)

- **Measurement:** Sample efficiency ratio (affective vs. tabula-rasa), framing effect size.
- **Success criterion:** ≥50% sample efficiency improvement, framed vs. unframed effect p < 0.01.
