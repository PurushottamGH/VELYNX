## Executive conclusion

Project P1 is engineering-rich but scientifically early. No computational mechanism of intelligence is validated, and no P1 theory is supported. The program has a locally reproducible exploratory assay and a usable scientific foundation, but the present M1 apparatus is not yet an immutable or confirmatory evidence source: it is absent from the committed revision, required automation does not yet assess the live apparatus, and non-clean runs are not machine-excluded.

The highest-confidence present findings are scoped negative or methodological:

1. legacy exact-keyword benchmark success did not survive keyword-free paraphrase;
2. the tested legacy free-energy consolidation policy produced no measurable R1 PredictionRMSE advantage over null;
3. a historical confidence emitter produced a hard `CERTAIN` fabrication failure;
4. the inspected E0 execution did not instantiate its growth treatment and therefore did not test H*;
5. P1 v0/M0 is a deterministic Discovery assay, but its replay and surprise contrasts are causally confounded.

## 1. What P1 currently knows

### 1.1 Semantic benchmark

The 32-item EXP-0 paired test found:

- Tier-2 legacy originals: `32/32`;
- Tier-2 keyword-free paraphrases: `0/32`;
- McNemar exact `p=4.656612873077393e-10`;
- paired bootstrap difference CI `[1.0, 1.0]`.

This rejects the inference that the tested exact-keyword benchmark demonstrated paraphrase-robust semantic understanding.

### 1.2 Legacy free-energy consolidation

R1 comprised 72 runs over six policies, two replay horizons, two noise levels, and three seeds. PredictionRMSE was:

- FreeEnergyPolicy: `0.2991 ± 0.0976`;
- null: `0.2951 ± 0.0975`;
- effect: `+0.0040`;
- `p=0.8660`;
- 95% CI `[-0.1163, 0.1251]`.

No measurable advantage was detected. The interval does not establish equivalence. Five of six primary capability metrics were unavailable.

### 1.3 Calibration failure

A historical six-query probe recorded a fabricated irrelevant answer with confidence `CERTAIN`. This falsifies any claim that the tested historical emitter was uniformly honest. It does not estimate population calibration and therefore leaves the broader calibration hypothesis open.

### 1.4 E0 treatment failure

The recovered E0 aggregate contained three seeds and recorded zero growth events in all conditions. Its diagnostic states that a per-symbol entropy delta was compared with a total-data-code penalty, making firing impossible in that execution.

Scientific ruling: E0 is invalid as a test of H*. H* is untested, not rejected. Repetition is scientifically stopped until trigger operability is established with known controls.

Report initial mastery, final oracle-relative prior-task loss, and plasticity. Use the result only to decide whether surprise merits Validation.

### H* line

Do not rerun E0. First calibrate the growth trigger on known compressible and incompressible streams under coherent units. Only after positive and negative controls pass may E0 resume.

### Calibration line

Freeze a representative adjudicated query set and run EXP-1 against constant-confidence and retrieval baselines with a zero-tolerance hard-fabrication criterion.

### Affective line

Run only on third-party tasks frozen independently of the affect map, with objective outcomes and neutral prompts.

### Instrument line

Run Experiment Zero when a validated experimental pipeline is required. A pass licenses only that the pipeline detects known carrier dependence and rejects named impostors.
