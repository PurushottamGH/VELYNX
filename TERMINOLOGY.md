### Variables and Operations
* **[FACT] Capacity Growth ($g$):** The addition of parameters to the predictive model, triggered strictly by persistent prediction error satisfying an MDL threshold. (Replaces "learning" or "development").
* **[FACT] MDL Trigger ($G$):** Minimum Description Length threshold. Growth occurs if and only if $G = H_{before} - H_{after} - \lambda_{model} > 0$.
* **[FACT] Log-Loss ($L$):** The proper scoring rule $L = −log P_θ(x_{t+1} | x_{≤t})$ replacing the arbitrary energy equation $E = \lambda H + \mu S + \nu A$.
* **[FACT] Emergence Statistic ($M(\theta_k)$):** Normalized mutual information (NMI) between the learned latent partition and the true latent generator, referenced against a shuffled-input control to prove the structure is not merely injected by the designer.
* **[FACT] Calibration:** The degree to which reported uncertainty tiers (CERTAIN, PROBABLE, DEBATED, UNKNOWN) match their empirical likelihood of correctness (ECE < 0.1).
* **[FACT] Affective Indexing:** The use of an emotional or physiological scalar value to index and retrieve procedural problem-solving schemas.
