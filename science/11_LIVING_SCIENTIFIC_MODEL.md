- **Model version:** 1.2.0

- **Purpose:** State what P1 knows, does not know, and must test next without converting implementation or narrative into evidence

## 1. Present scientific position

P1 does not yet possess a validated computational principle of intelligence. It possesses:

1. a locally reproducible but provenance-limited, scientifically provisional continual-learning assay (`EXP-2026-0003`);
2. two valid scoped legacy negative results (R1 and EXP-0);
3. one hard historical calibration failure;
4. one invalid E0 execution that established treatment non-operability but did not test H*;
5. several explicit, discriminable hypotheses awaiting controlled experiments;
6. no supported integrative theory.

The current scientific opportunity is not to add mechanisms. It is to convert a few narrow signals into identifiable causal contrasts and to remove measurement defects capable of inverting conclusions.

## 2. Most reliable current findings

### 2.1 Exact-keyword success did not survive paraphrase

For the tested 32-concept Tier-2 detector, performance was 32/32 on original exact-keyword prompts and 0/32 on keyword-free paraphrases. The paired result is complete and statistically precise for that set. This rejects the inference that the legacy benchmark demonstrated paraphrase-robust semantic understanding.

It does not prove that all P1 systems lack semantics.

### 2.2 The tested legacy free-energy policy had no measured R1 advantage

Across R1's 72 runs, FreeEnergyPolicy did not improve PredictionRMSE over null: treatment-minus-null `+0.0040`, `p=0.8660`, CI `[-0.1163, 0.1251]`. The finding is a valid negative result against the claimed tested advantage. The interval is too wide for equivalence.

Five of six declared primary metrics were unavailable, which is itself a major finding about the legacy measurement substrate.

### 2.3 The historical uncertainty emitter violated a hard honesty condition

One legacy probe returned fabricated irrelevant content and labeled it `CERTAIN`. This directly contradicts any universal claim that the historical tiers were already calibrated or honest. It does not estimate calibration over a population and therefore does not decide whether a revised system can satisfy H1.

### 2.4 E0 did not test error-gated structure acquisition

The inspected three-seed E0 execution recorded zero growth events because the trigger compared a per-symbol entropy improvement with a total-data-code penalty. Since the treatment did not occur, H* was neither supported nor rejected.

This is not “inconclusive because only three seeds” in the primary sense. Even infinitely many repetitions of an absent treatment would not test H*.

### 2.5 M0 is a Discovery assay, not replay mechanism evidence

The P1 v0 seed-0 values were independently reproduced locally, and nine rig tests passed. The successor M1 working tree also completed 202 local platform/rig tests with 88.5% line coverage and passed formatting and config-driven smoke checks. These observations support local engineering conformance, not immutable or confirmatory reproducibility: the apparatus is absent from the committed revision and the assessed smoke manifest is dirty.

The run shows that the assay can exhibit prior-task degradation and replay-associated differences.

The variants also differ in update count, decay applications, historical-data exposure, replay budget, immediate-sample rehearsal, and compute. Thus the only causal object supported by seed 0 is the whole bundled variant. Historical replay content and surprise timing remain unidentified.

## 4. Mechanism state

No mechanism is validated.

- Exact substring matching is established as lexical routing and rejected as semantic evidence.
- Legacy free-energy consolidation is rejected within its R1 test scope.
- Error-gated growth is scientifically blocked until operability is demonstrated.
- Historical replay remains a bundled exploratory intervention.

- Calibration and affective indexing remain open mechanism hypotheses.
- Historical-carrier memory has a strong preregistered discriminator but no execution evidence.

## 5. Critical assumptions

The most dangerous load-bearing assumptions are:

1. the forgetting scalar validly measures retained capability;
2. decay-induced forgetting tells us more than how replay compensates for artificial erasure;
3. learned structure can be separated from injected structure;
4. E0 can supply an environment that is nontrivial, non-seeded, and feasible;
5. calibration and affective experiments can use representative, non-circular evaluation data;
6. the PH-1 historical cue is both leak-free and feasibly retainable.

The designer-injection audit supplies credible opposing evidence to assumption 3 for the historical VELYNX instantiation.

## 6. Highest-priority unknowns

The ranking is driven by expected decision value, not conceptual prestige.

**Immediate readiness gate:** Can a committed, isolated M1 checkout reproduce identical decision-relevant outputs and complete artifacts while required automation tests the live apparatus and rejects non-clean confirmatory inputs? This is resolved before Cycle 001 because every downstream result depends on it. It is not a mechanism question.

After that gate:

1. Does the current forgetting outcome identify retention rather than weak initial mastery?
2. Does surprise timing outperform matched random timing?
3. Does historical replay help after update count, decay, compute, and immediate exposure are matched?
4. Can E0's growth treatment be made operable under coherent, non-arbitrary units?
5. Which legacy artifacts can be reused with reliable provenance?
6. Can the pipeline detect historical-carrier dependence and reject leakage/capacity alternatives?
7. Are Program A confidence tiers calibrated across intended deployment strata?
8. Does affective indexing improve independent objective outcomes?
9. Do any later validated mechanisms transfer beyond constructed environments?

## 8. Negative knowledge retained

P1 has learned that:

- adding a sophisticated consolidation objective did not produce a measured R1 PredictionRMSE advantage;
- exact-keyword benchmark success can vanish completely under paraphrase;
- an uncertainty system can confidently emit fabricated content despite an honesty narrative;
- an experiment can be fully implemented and still fail to instantiate its treatment;
- deterministic replay demonstrations can remain causally uninterpretable;
- historical authority labels do not substitute for evidence validity.

These are durable scientific results and constraints on future design, not embarrassing artifacts to delete.

## 9. Theory status

No P1 theory meets the Theory Registry standard. There are not yet two independently validated constituent hypotheses, an integrated explanatory relation that outperforms alternatives, and a successful novel risky prediction.

T-02 is a useful specification discipline, not a supported theory. H*, replay, surprise, calibration, affective indexing, and carrier dependence remain separate hypotheses with different scopes.

## 10. Smallest experiment that would most reduce uncertainty

Before a scientific pilot, the smallest readiness test is two isolated executions of one frozen deterministic config from a clean committed checkout, with required CI exercising M1 tests, frozen-rig equivalence, config-to-artifact execution, and the live artifact/reproducibility paths. Decision-relevant metrics and artifact hashes must agree under a declared normalization policy, and a deliberately dirty confirmatory input must be rejected. This does not validate any scientific construct; it establishes that the apparatus can produce admissible exploratory evidence.

After that gate, the smallest useful scientific experiment for the active v0 line is an exploratory matched-random replay-timing control paired to SurpriseGate while simultaneously reporting:

- initial mastery after each task;
- final prior-task oracle-relative excess loss;
- current-task adaptation/plasticity;
- identical realized replay counts and batch-size profiles.

This single experiment can cheaply determine whether surprise timing merits a full validation program. A null or random-superior result would prevent unnecessary mechanism proliferation.

For the H* line, the next action is smaller than E0: a positive/negative control calibration of growth-trigger units. No H* run should occur before it passes.

## 11. Current scientific conclusion

P1's strongest state is methodological and negative rather than mechanistic. The program has successfully exposed several false or unsupported inferences and now has a traceable set of falsifiable questions. Its near-term success criterion is not a positive intelligence result. It is obtaining one clean, resource-matched, measurement-valid causal contrast whose adverse outcome would actually change the roadmap.
