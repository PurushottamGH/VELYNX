## Readiness gate versus scientific priority

`UNK-2026-0010` is a short, blocking apparatus question rather than a competing research line. It is resolved first because a failure would make every downstream result unreconstructable. Once that gate passes, forgetting-construct validity remains the highest-priority scientific unknown.

## Why the top scientific unknown is measurement, not mechanism

If the primary outcome can improve when a model learned less initially, every replay comparison can be directionally misleading. Resolving this construct defect has wider decision impact than collecting more seeds under the same scalar.

## Smallest uncertainty-reducing experiment

The cheapest direct mechanism discriminator is an equal-budget random replay schedule matched to surprise. However, the primary metric must simultaneously report initial mastery and final oracle-relative loss; otherwise the experiment may efficiently answer the wrong question.

Therefore the minimal combined next step is:

1. augment the existing analysis with initial mastery and final prior-task excess loss;
2. add matched random replay timing with the exact surprise replay-count profile;
3. keep the run explicitly exploratory;
4. use the outcome only to decide whether a full validation protocol is worth running.

## Deferred unknowns

External transfer and unified theory formation are intentionally deferred. They are important but have low current feasibility and cannot repair within-assay nonidentifiability. P1 should not generalize a mechanism it has not yet identified.
