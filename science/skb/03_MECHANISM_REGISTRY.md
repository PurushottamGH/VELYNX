## Mechanism-by-mechanism scientific state

### Legacy free-energy consolidation

The R1 result gives no measurable PredictionRMSE advantage over null. Because the test's FinalEnergy outcome is partly circular with the policy objective, it does not rescue the mechanism. The record is a scoped negative result, not a universal judgment on all active-inference or free-energy approaches.

### Exact-substring concept detection

EXP-0 establishes that the detector's apparent success depended on exact lexical presence in the tested set. It remains a legitimate lexical routing implementation but is rejected as evidence of semantic understanding.

### Error-gated growth

No valid causal claim exists because the inspected E0 treatment never occurred. Scientific work must not proceed directly to more seeds. The smallest prior requirement is metrological: show that the trigger activates on a known compressible stream and remains inactive on a known negative control under coherent units.

### Uncertainty tiers

The Q6 observation is a hard failure for the historical emitter. The mechanism may be revised or replaced, but implementation changes do not count as evidence. It earns support only through calibration and honesty outcomes on frozen adjudicated data.

### Affective indexing

There is no accepted evidence that affective framing improves objective performance. The mechanism must be separated from the authored 32-concept graph and evaluated on tasks frozen independently of the mapping.

### Reservoir replay

M0 demonstrates the whole replay bundle can change measured outcomes. It does not isolate stored historical information from extra updates, altered decay frequency, current-observation rehearsal, or resource expenditure.

### Surprise gate

The gate's threshold crossings are correlated with loss, task switches, rare transitions, and underfit. Until matched random timing is tested, “surprise” is a label for a schedule, not an identified informative cause.

### Historical carrier

This is the only currently preregistered mechanism with a direct lesion-style discriminator: erase or permute the carrier while preserving current-input marginals. Even a positive result would establish dependence on a historical carrier, not where or how the memory is physically represented.

## Complexity rule

No mechanism may advance from Proposed/Exploratory because it is elegant, biologically suggestive, or already coded. It advances only when its unique causal contribution is separated from simpler alternatives at matched resources.
