## 3. Dependency bottlenecks

### Apparatus-admissibility bottleneck

`ASM-0010/0011` + `UNK-0010` + `SDEBT-0010/0011/0012` block confirmatory evidence from the M1 platform and delay Cycle 001 execution. This is an immediate, narrow gate; it does not change mechanism beliefs.

### Measurement bottleneck

`ASM-0002` + `UNK-0003` + `SDEBT-0003/0006` jointly limit both replay hypotheses. After apparatus readiness, this remains the widest scientific bottleneck.

### Identifiability bottleneck

`SDEBT-0001/0002` prevent attribution to historical replay or surprise. More seeds alone do not resolve it.

### Treatment bottleneck

`UNK-0005` + `SDEBT-0009` block H* before statistical power matters.

### Injection bottleneck

`ASM-0005` + `EVD-0008` + `SDEBT-0007` block any emergence claim based on the audited legacy system.

### Provenance bottleneck

`UNK-0004` + `SDEBT-0004/0005/0010` limit reuse of legacy and M0 records. SKB v1.0 reduces this by separating sources, observations, evidence, and decisions.

## 4. Roadmap implications

1. Delay Discovery Cycle 001 execution until the committed M1 apparatus passes clean-checkout CI, live-surface integrity/reproducibility checks, and repeated-run identity; keep all resulting pilot evidence exploratory.
2. Do not run confirmatory replay studies until non-clean evidence is machine-excluded and preregistration, metric, matched-control, seed, and resource requirements are frozen.
3. Do not scale E0 until trigger metrology passes.
4. After the apparatus gate, run the matched-random surprise control as a cheap Discovery discriminator.
5. Run Experiment Zero when pipeline calibration is needed before higher-stakes experiments.
6. Keep calibration and affective indexing as separate bounded lines; neither licenses a general P1 theory.
7. Defer transfer and theory integration until at least one within-scope mechanism validates.

## 5. What a new scientist should believe on day one

- P1 is scientifically early despite extensive engineering and documentation.
- Exact-keyword semantics and tested free-energy advantage have scoped negative evidence.
- H* has not yet received a valid test.
- Replay is a promising but confounded signal; surprise has not been isolated.
- Calibration has a historical hard failure; affective indexing has no accepted evidence.
- The program's current strength is its improved ability to reject bad inference and preserve negative knowledge.
