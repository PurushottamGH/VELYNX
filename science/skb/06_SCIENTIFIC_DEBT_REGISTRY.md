## Blocking interpretation

- **B1:** Discovery can continue, but interpretation is narrowed.
- **B2:** Exploratory runs may continue; validation claims cannot be accepted.
- **B3:** Major program or theory decisions cannot proceed.
- **B4:** Further runs of the same design predictably produce uninterpretable evidence and must stop.
