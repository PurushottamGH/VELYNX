## Decision rationales

### Legacy hypothesis reduction

The migration retains hypotheses only when they can fail under a feasible discriminating experiment. The legacy prohibition on new hypotheses is not adopted; a living research program must revise its hypothesis set when evidence exposes better alternatives.

### Semantic benchmark rejection

The rejected object is the inference from the benchmark, not the general possibility of semantics. Exact lexical success followed by complete keyword-free collapse is incompatible with the claimed robustness.

### Free-energy policy rejection

The rejection is scoped. R1 does not establish equivalence, but it removes the claimed measured advantage and makes further complexity unjustified absent a new discriminating reason.

### E0 no-verdict ruling

A treatment that never activates cannot falsify the target. The decision preserves H* as open while placing a scientific stop on repeating the same invalid design.

### Calibration no-verdict ruling

A hard failure blocks claims that the historical emitter was honest. It does not kill the broader hypothesis that a retrieval system can be calibrated.

### M0 acceptance

M0 meets the Discovery standard for a useful assay: deterministic execution, measurable outcomes, and a controllable phenomenon. It does not meet Validation requirements for causal replay or surprise interpretation.

### Replay validation revision

The next milestone is not “more seeds” alone. More observations of a confounded contrast increase precision around an unidentified bundle. Controls must first make the scientific question identifiable.

### T-01/T-02 revision

T-02's stateful form contains T-01's memory requirement and supplies no distinct risky prediction. Keeping both as competing hypotheses would create false complexity. T-02 is retained as a measurement discipline.

### Legacy migration ruling

Terms such as `FACT`, `ACCEPTED`, `KEEP`, and `canonical` are preserved in source documents but translated only through explicit evidence-validity and Decision records.

### M1 scientific-readiness ruling

The engineering report was substantially corroborated. The M1 working tree passes extensive local conformance checks, so the scientific designs are not rejected. However, the platform is not part of the committed revision, committed CI does not exercise it, two green gates target legacy surfaces, and non-clean runs are not machine-excluded. Discovery Cycle 001 remains approved in purpose but execution is delayed until the minimum reconstructability and gate-sensitivity conditions pass. Confirmatory evidence remains prohibited until those gates plus all pre-existing preregistration, measurement, causal-control, and statistical requirements are satisfied.

## Reversibility

Every decision has a reversal trigger in `SKB_RECORDS_v1.0.yaml`. Reversal does not delete the earlier decision; it creates a successor record explaining the new evidence and changed scope.
